- Verify that no two flowers share the same zone ID.
- Provide real-time feedback: highlight violating flowers in a subtle translucent red state if a rule is broken.
- Trigger a win condition when all N flowers are placed correctly without rule violations.

- `GameScreen`: Holds the overall level state, active flower counter (e.g., "Flowers 3/5"), lives/hearts counter, and rule indicators.
